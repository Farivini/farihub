# xlsx-bridge

Built from `native/xlsx-bridge` (this repository) with `native/xlsx-bridge/build.sh`.

It links IronCalc 0.9.1 (`ironcalc` for XLSX import/export, `ironcalc_base` for the
workbook model), licensed MIT OR Apache-2.0 (https://github.com/ironcalc/IronCalc),
and their Rust dependencies (zip, quick-xml, chrono, bitcode, serde, wasm-bindgen and
others listed in `native/xlsx-bridge/Cargo.lock`), all under MIT and/or Apache-2.0
licenses or compatible permissive licenses.
